About Us

A Family Trade, Rolled in Estelí.

Don Cubano is a family-owned, Canadian wholesale cigar distributor based in Windsor, Ontario. Since 2008 we have worked directly with manufacturers in Estelí, Nicaragua, blending cigars with tobacco sourced from across four nations for the Canadian retail market.
